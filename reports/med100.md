# med100: medicine corpus 30 to 100 products (gate FAILED, not merged)

**Result: main stays at 30 products.** On the fresh 100-name test, 2 of 25 decoys
(digoxin, bisoprolol fumarate) surfaced a review candidate. The gate allows 0.
Nothing was loosened or retuned. The 100-product corpus, names and raw scores are on
branch `med100` (corpus at 290296c, names at d4d3bbe, scores `results/med100_scores.jsonl`).

## What was added
70 new DailyMed human-medicine SPLs, pinned by hash, same parser, aliases and labeler
(SPL labeler) as before: 100 products, 2440 chunks, 327 sourced aliases. Names came from
ClinCalc "Top 300 of 2024" (https://clincalc.com/DrugStats/Top300Drugs.aspx, MEPS data),
ranks 1 to 200, list committed first (6d8f04f). The run stopped at 100, so the OTC names
at the end of the list were never fetched: the 70 are nearly all Rx, mostly repackager
labels. Two combination labels slipped through my one-generic-name check (olmesartan
triple combination, pioglitazone with metformin); Eliquis and Zetia are brand labels.

## Fetch failures (4 of 74 attempts)
hydrochlorothiazide and valsartan hit an already pinned product; apixaban and
lamotrigine had a first result with several product names (the next result passed).

Positives: 49/75 confidently correct (65%, Wilson 95% 54 to 75%), 0 wrong matches.
Decoys: 23/25 rejected; the 2 review candidates were digoxin (cosine 0.677) and
bisoprolol fumarate (0.661). With 100 products, generic words ("tablets", "fumarate")
push look-alike cosines over 0.65. Raising the threshold would trade away positives
and was not done.

Latency (100 queries, CPU, uncontended re-run, identical outcomes 100/100): median 1.20 s,
p90 1.32 s. The first scoring run shared the CPU with an index build (median 6.9 s) and
is not the number to quote. Matcher index (100 documents, bge-large, CPU): about 110 s.
The 2440-chunk index for product questions was stopped unfinished at 12 of 39 batches
(about 2 min per batch, so roughly 80 min); it was not needed once the gate failed.

## What the numbers do not show
Names are authored by me, not photo OCR. Brand names come from my own knowledge, with no
brand map in the system, so misspelled and brand misses are expected. Only 25 decoys:
0 of 25 would still allow a population error rate up to 13%, and we observed 2 of 25
(Wilson 95% 2 to 25%). Correctness is by expected product name, not graded by an LLM
or human. Product questions (askPublic) were not evaluated at 100 products.
