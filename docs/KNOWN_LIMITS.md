# Known limits

The README keeps a short summary. This file is the full list.

## Full list of known limits

Numbers below come from the reports named in brackets. Every relevance grade in this project is an LLM grade (claude-sonnet-5-5) except a 308-row human validation sample (`results/step13_llm_vs_human.md`); nothing here is a compliance determination.

- **Real-photo extraction is weak.** CV3 on 30 new real device-package photos (10 test): product name 0/10, lot 5/7, expiry 7/8, with the OCR models not upgraded (`reports/cv3.md`). The CV is otherwise tuned and scored on synthetic labels of one template family; very hard photos can make clean labels fail with confident false alarms; photos that are too blurry are rejected with a retake request.
- **CV2 field regressions.** On the 10 previously exposed real photos CV2 lowered lot reads from 7 to 4 and left product name at 0 (`reports/parth_cv2.md`). CV2 also needs the Tesseract executable; without it the profile falls back to the baseline.
- **Print verification is measured on simulated photos only.** Template mode: 46/48 correct prints confirmed, 0/48 false matches, 2 hard prints sent to rescan (`reports/print_template_mode.md`). Real print photos were not measured. Symbols are not compared in print verification.
- **Symbols are not measured on real photos.** The symbol detector is not trusted: symbol checks always ask a human. Real carton photos were not read (`docs/CV_STATUS.md`).
- **Abstention is weak.** On the 13-query test split no score reaches AUROC 0.90 or precision 0.80 (best 0.800 AUROC); a different 20-query set looked better (hybrid top AUROC 0.985) and was not confirmed (`results/step18_final.md`).
- **The AI answer is slow and optional.** Extractive passages are the default. The generative 3b answer answered 70% of answerable queries at a median of 38.3 s, with no wrong answers on the 10 no_answer queries; the sentence-selection mode failed its frozen gate and stays off (`reports/rag_select.md`). The AI answer needs Ollama, and always falls back to the passages.
- **The reranked display is a small gain on a small test.** Held-out hit rate 9/15 vs 8/15 (one query), median latency 1.04 s vs 0.37 s, 1 of 5 no_answer queries still shows quotes; LLM grades, one grader, 20 queries (`reports/rag_rerank.md`). Costs about 1 s per ask and an 87 MB model.
- **Medicine corpus is 30 products** (DailyMed, 618 chunks). Name matching: 31 of 45 fresh names confidently correct, 10 sent to review, 4 rejected, and 15 of 15 decoys rejected; authored names are not photo-OCR accuracy (`reports/medicine_aliases.md`). Medicine mode stays at 30 products: the 100-product expansion failed its gate and was not merged (`reports/med100.md`). The manufacturer is the SPL labeler, not necessarily the physical manufacturer. Brand names without an alias do not match their generics. Photo scan: see the next limit.
- **Live DailyMed lookup (CR-28) needs internet and a person's choice.** Fresh set of 30 products and 10 decoys, scored once: right label listed 29/30, passages with verified citations shown 27/30, 0 wrong-product answers, 10/10 decoys rejected, median fetch-to-answer 31 s but up to 356 s for large Rx labels on a busy CPU. The picker in the test was a script, correct spellings only, answer relevance not graded, no fuzzy matching ("Prevacid 24HR" found nothing). Offline it says so and the curated 30 keep working (`reports/live_dailymed.md`).
- **Exports are DRAFT only.** Generated labels always carry the DRAFT mark, even after approval (no approved variant is rendered). Identifiers are the GS1 test placeholder, and a template with a background image is refused (D-5).
- **Public photo scan is real but slow, and a person confirms it.** It never auto-confirms: a match asks "Is this X?" with the evidence, and label information appears only after Confirm (buttons Confirm, Not this one, Type the name). It identifies mainly through the NDC printed on the package or read from its barcode, looked up exactly in a table of 100 package NDCs from the 30 curated labels; when no table NDC is found, full-label text matching (method E) is the fallback, and only then may a short review list be shown. It can take up to a minute. Gate run on 57 positive photos (15 products) and 99 decoy photos (33 decoy products): positives correct 46/57 (Wilson 69 to 89%) vs 34/57 for text matching alone, 0 wrong on positives, 0 wrong decoys on 45 photos (0 to 8%) and on 54 more (0 to 7%), 6 timeouts; a printed or barcode NDC in the table was present on 33/57 positives and on none of the 99 decoy photos (`reports/ndc_scan.md`, `reports/public_scan.md`). A single-reader re-read of the positives gave 42/57. The photos are DailyMed package-label images (the 145 pinned images and photos rendered from them) with simulated camera conditions, not real phone photos; real phone photos are unmeasured. When nothing is identified, the page now looks the read name up on DailyMed (see the photo name lookup limit above).
- **Demo photos are package artwork, not phone photos.** Every photo in `demo_photos/` is DailyMed package artwork with simulated camera conditions (clean, moderate, hard), or a synthetic image drawn with Pillow (`12_synthetic_lopressor.jpg`, placeholder NDC). Real phone photos are unmeasured.
- **The live-label gate is a probe, not an evaluation.** Live DailyMed labels use a score gate of 0.02 (`LABEL_GATE` in `src/api/live_dailymed.py`), set from one label and 8 probe questions. It is conservative (a real miss says "This label doesn't seem to answer that."), but it was not tuned or evaluated. The curated 30 have no gate: a 0.02 gate would fold 12 of 18 scored on-topic questions on 3 products (re-measured with `scripts/measure_curated_gate.py`), so they show "Closest passages from the label" instead.
- **Photo name lookup on DailyMed is strict and was tuned on the evaluation photos.** When a photo has no NDC in the table and no curated match, the page takes a word next to a strength ("50 mg") from the text it read and proposes the first DailyMed label whose product or generic name starts with it, always as "Is this X?" with Confirm, Not this one and Type the name. On the recorded OCR lines of the scan evaluation (photos the scan did not identify) it proposed the right medicine for 8 of 39 decoy photos and 4 of 11 positives, wrong for 0, and nothing for the rest (`ui/tools/check_photo_lookup_live.cjs`); the rule was adjusted on these same lines, so the numbers are optimistic.
- **AI mode is slow on CPU.** The optional AI answer took about 14 to 15 s with the model loaded and 44.6 s with a cold model (measured once each), 56.7 s on a busy PC; the pages wait up to 180 s and then show the passages with a note.
- **Template registration checks shape and layout only** (slot geometry and the DRAFT band).
- **No multi-side scan.** One label side per upload.
- **Publishing is local:** the page and QR point at this server (`LABELLENS_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`), they are not hosted anywhere.
- **LLM-graded labels.** Retrieval and RAG numbers use LLM relevance grades with wide intervals; the LLM is stricter than humans at the 0/1 boundary (`results/step13_llm_vs_human.md`).
- **Mocks:** none. All 42 operations of `docs/contracts/openapi.yaml` are implemented for real (41 in `REAL_OPERATIONS`, plus `getHealth`; a mock response would carry `X-Mock: true`). `demo_e2e.py` lists any stage that hit a mock or a fallback.
